Dice

Loaded dice

Switch a die type on, then drag its bars to change how often each face comes up. A face at 10 lands ten times as often as a face at 1. Dice with a fixed result ignore these weights.

Interaction

Tap a die to re-roll itOff

The die jumps high with a random direction and spin. If it hits other dice they react with real physics, so their results can change too. Re-rolls are pure physics: fixed results and weights don't apply to them.

Lock settled diceOff

Resting dice become immovable. A re-rolled die still bounces off them, but their results never change. Needs “Tap a die to re-roll it”.

Display

Hide all buttons and resultsOff

Just the table and the dice, for streaming or a display screen. Only this settings button stays, in the top-right corner. Tap the table or press Space to roll.

Quality

High resolutionOff

On by default. Full device resolution, 8× antialiasing, double-resolution textures, smoother dice edges, and sharper shadows and reflections. Turn it off on slower devices to save memory and battery. Saved on this device, separate from the link.
